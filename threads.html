What it posts

Posts the video and caption from your inbox to a Threads account you own. Same inbox + sidecar + go command as the other browser platforms.

Who it is for

People who own a Threads account and want to post from their machine.

What you need before connect

  • A Threads account you own, signed in in regular Chrome.
  • Google Chrome installed.
  • Project folder cloned from GitHub.
  • No Business conversion. Threads has no Business-account rule in this tool.

How you connect

Run the login helper and sign into Threads in the window it opens:

python3 setup_playwright_logins.py

More: Setup guide.

Everyday use

Drop a video and a matching .json sidecar into inbox/ (same base name). Run ./posting_tool_go.command or python3 schedule_batch.py dispatch. Do not run two dispatches at once. Check posted/ or failed/ + screenshots.

Beginner walkthrough: Setup guide → Everyday use.

Limits / account-type warnings

No Business-account rule on Threads. If a login expires, run the login helper again and sign back in. Never paste secrets into email or an AI.
