How it works

From clone to scheduled posts. You own the accounts. The tool runs on your machine.

Clone + install

git clone https://github.com/okwithit9-debug/the-posting-tool, then follow the Setup guide.

Connect accounts

Log in to the platforms you use on your own machine. Nothing is sent to us.

Schedule & post

Videos land in the posting inbox and go out across the platforms you use.

Setup is written for beginners. Use ChatGPT, Claude, or Grok with the Setup guide — that’s the fastest path.
Instagram, TikTok, and Pinterest — Business accounts required. Convert each of those accounts to Business before you connect. Personal accounts will look broken. That is a platform limit, not a defect. Pinterest Business is what lets scheduling work. TikTok Business is what unlocks the link in bio on new accounts and accounts under 1k followers. Instagram can be Business or Creator. Look for the Schedule content switch. If a Professional account is missing it, the tool still posts now. That is not a broken tool. FAQ

Common questions.

What is The Posting Tool?

A free, open-source, self-hosted multi-platform poster. You drop a video and a caption file in the inbox, and it schedules or posts to X, Threads, TikTok, Pinterest, YouTube, and Instagram from your own machine.

Is it really free?

Yes. It is MIT licensed. There is no paid version, no subscription, and no account with us.

Where is the code?

The Posting Tool: github.com/okwithit9-debug/the-posting-tool. Sara AI: github.com/okwithit9-debug/sara-ai. Open Source Clipper: github.com/okwithit9-debug/open-source-clipper. All three live together at github.com/okwithit9-debug/open-source.

Is Instagram included?

Yes. Instagram is one of the six supported platforms.

Instagram, TikTok, and Pinterest all need a Business account (Instagram can also be Creator). Convert before you connect. On Instagram, Personal accounts cannot use the tool. If a Professional (Business or Creator) account is missing the Schedule content switch, the tool still posts now. Instagram often hides the switch for days after convert. That is not a broken tool. See the Setup guide for the checkpoint.

Do TikTok and Pinterest need business accounts too?

Yes. Pinterest needs Business to schedule. TikTok needs Business for the link in bio if the account is new or under 1k followers. Convert before you connect. A personal account is a platform limit, not a defect.

How do I set this up if I’m not technical?

Setup is written for beginners. Use ChatGPT, Claude, or Grok with the Setup guide — that’s the fastest path.

What does support cover?

Start with the Setup guide and its AI prompts (install prompt, then the AI fix prompt if something fails). If you are still stuck, open a GitHub Issue.

How do I contribute?

Open an issue or a pull request on GitHub. Bug reports, platform fixes, and docs improvements are all welcome.

Is this hosted SaaS?

No. You run the tool on your own machine (or your own host). You keep the accounts, and the code is yours to read and change.
