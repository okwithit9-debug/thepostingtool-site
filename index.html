Run it yourself

Clone it. Connect it. Post.

Prefer to drive it yourself? The code runs on your machine and your accounts never leave it.

How it works

  1. Clone + install: git clone https://github.com/okwithit9-debug/the-posting-tool, then follow the Setup guide.
  2. Connect accounts: log in to the platforms you use on your own machine. Nothing is sent to us.
  3. Schedule & post: videos land in the posting inbox and go out across the platforms you use.

Setup is written for beginners. Use ChatGPT, Claude, or Grok with the Setup guide, that's the fastest path.

⚠ Business accounts required

Instagram, TikTok and Pinterest need a Business account (Instagram can also be Creator). Convert before you connect. Personal accounts will look broken; that's a platform limit, not a bug. If Instagram hides the Schedule content switch, the tool still posts now. Details: Setup guide → Business accounts.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is The Posting Tool?

A free, open-source, self-hosted multi-platform poster. You drop a video and a caption file in the inbox, and it schedules or posts to X, Threads, TikTok, Pinterest, YouTube, and Instagram from your own machine.

Is it really free?

Yes. It is MIT licensed. There is no paid version, no subscription, and no account with us.

Where is the code?

The Posting Tool: github.com/okwithit9-debug/the-posting-tool. Sara AI: github.com/okwithit9-debug/sara-ai. Open Source Clipper: github.com/okwithit9-debug/open-source-clipper. Home AI lab security dashboard: open-source/cybersecurity/Node dashboard. All of them live together at github.com/okwithit9-debug/open-source.

Is Instagram included?

Yes. Instagram is one of the six supported platforms. It needs a Business or Creator account; Personal accounts cannot use the tool. If a Professional account is missing the Schedule content switch, the tool still posts now. See the Setup guide for the checkpoint.

Do TikTok and Pinterest need business accounts too?

Yes. Pinterest needs Business to schedule. TikTok needs Business for the link in bio if the account is new or under 1k followers. Convert before you connect.

How do I set this up if I'm not technical?

Ask the Grok Bot, or use ChatGPT, Claude, or Grok with the Setup guide.

Where do I get help?

Start with the Setup guide and its AI prompts. Still stuck? Open a GitHub Issue or email support@thepostingtool.com.

How do I contribute?

Open an issue or a pull request on GitHub. Bug reports, platform fixes, and docs improvements are all welcome.

Is this hosted SaaS?

No. You run the tool on your own machine (or your own host). You keep the accounts, and the code is yours to read and change.
